# ROKO 4 EVER - Tournament Rules

## General Rules

1. All players must use their own account.
2. No hacking or cheating allowed.
3. Emulator players are not allowed.
4. Teaming with opponents is prohibited.
5. Abuse and toxic behavior are prohibited.

## Match Rules

1. Join room before match start.
2. Late entry is not allowed.
3. Follow admin instructions.
4. Screenshot proof may be required.

## Prize Rules

1. Prize will be credited after result verification.
2. Fake claims will result in account suspension.
3. Withdrawal requests are subject to verification.

## Penalties

1. Warning
2. Temporary Ban
3. Permanent Ban
